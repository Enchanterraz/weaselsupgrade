Burn
Permanent · irreversible · separate from staking
Burning destroys a weasel forever. It is not staking. It cannot be undone by you, by us, or by anyone. In exchange you receive a permanent share of the burn pool, which earns a slice of royalties for as long as the collection trades.

Your share is diluted every time someone else burns. Rewards depend entirely on trading volume — they may be small for a long time, or forever. Do not burn a weasel you would rather own.
